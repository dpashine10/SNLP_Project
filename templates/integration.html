Content-Type: application/json

{
  "query": "natural language processing",
  "top_k": 5,
  "include_evidence": true
}

The bridge must not send the email text, student ID, crisis details, or S3T confidence values.

Response contract

Evidence-bearing results

  • rank, name, author_id
  • score and confidence.level
  • Top supporting papers with title, year, and similarity

The service returns OpenAlex publication matches, not a verified university teaching roster or mentor-availability list.

How Group 7 ranks

Publication relevance, with inspectable evidence

The default strategy averages each researcher's three strongest matching papers and discounts papers with many co-authors. The result is not ranked by most recent publication, most published author, unique expertise, teaching assignment, or mentor availability. Reviewers can inspect the matched-paper count, publication years, similarity scores, related indexed topics, and the ranking-confidence explanation before deciding what to do.

Responsible AI

What the system can and cannot decide

  • Advisory only: it must not grade, discipline, determine eligibility, or automatically contact a student.
  • Human oversight: reviewers confirm the triage, risk signals, course mapping, and researcher evidence.
  • Confidence caution: Group 7 confidence bands help prioritize review; they are not calibrated probabilities.
  • Terminology caution: call the output “researchers whose papers match the topic,” not confirmed faculty or mentors.

Known limitations

Say these limitations aloud

  • Group 6 uses generated messages; real-student performance is unknown.
  • Keyword signals can miss crisis language and can mishandle negation.
  • Only the first catalogued course or topic is searched; course-code mappings still require registrar validation.
  • Group 7 is based on publication similarity and has no teaching-roster verification.
Integration reminder: Group 7 stays a separate HTTP service. Keep the Group 6 and Group 7 environments separate because they have different Python and dependency requirements.
