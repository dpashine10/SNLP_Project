Methodology & Rubric Transparency

Candidates were pooled across all six frozen Week 3 and Week 4 runs and scored on a 4-level rubric (3 = highly relevant, 2 = relevant, 1 = peripheral, 0 = not relevant; binary metrics threshold at ≥ 2). Per the Week 5 evaluation audit, pooled labels were produced via rubric-guided AI review with explicit provisional flags rather than dual human annotation, and unjudged candidates surfaced by top_k_average_author_discount are reported on condensed lists rather than penalized as false negatives.

NLP Concepts & Architecture Showcase

How our three-stage NLP pipeline combines dense vector search, non-autoregressive decision classification, and grounded generative reasoning across the standalone asset and university bridge.

Stage 1 · Retrieval

Dense Bi-Encoder (MiniLM)

Maps queries and publications into an asymmetric \(\mathbb{R}^{384}\) dense embedding space. Retrieval scales as \(O(N)\) dot products rather than expensive \(O(N \cdot L)\) cross-encoders. Researcher ranking aggregates candidate papers using our calibrated \(1/\sqrt{N_{\text{authors}}}\) co-author dilution penalty to prevent tie-crowding.

Stage 2 · Structured Decision

System-1 RLCD (Laya Model)

Uses convaiinnovations/laya with Reinforcement Learning from Calibrated Decisions (RLCD). Executes calibrated classification across three native primitives: choice (5-way methodology archetype & query domain), score (0–3 graded relevance rubric with abstention gate), and noul (\(P(\text{substantive publication})\) artifact filter).

Stage 3 · Grounded Reasoning

System-2 Grounded RAG (Groq / Gemini)

Synthesizes structured research answers strictly grounded in the retrieved OpenAlex dataset. Employs strict temperature control (\(T = 0.2\)) and requires explicit inline [Paper #k · DOI] citation tags. Features dual-provider failover (Groq Llama-3.3-70B ↔ Gemini 2.5 Flash) with deterministic extractive fallback.

Engineering Roadmap: 5 Identified Areas for Improvement

Concrete architectural and algorithmic enhancements to advance this implementation beyond baseline retrieval (excluding generic statements regarding real-world data access).

01

Domain-Adapted Citation-Graph Bi-Encoders (SPECTER2)

Replace general-domain web sentence encoders (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) with citation-proximity co-trained transformers such as allenai/specter2. By training on academic co-citation graphs, the embedding topology aligns with scholarly relatedness rather than superficial keyword and lexical co-occurrence.

02

Exponential Temporal Expertise Decay

Incorporate an exponential publication recency decay factor \(\lambda(t) = \exp(-\gamma \cdot [Y_{\text{current}} - Y_{\text{pub}}])\) into candidate scoring. Currently, a researcher's seminal paper from 2011 is weighted identically to their 2025 breakthrough; temporal weighting ensures active, contemporary topic specialists surface for advisor triage.

03

Authorship-Position Credit Assignment Priors

Transition from uniform \(1/\sqrt{N_{\text{authors}}}\) discounting to role-aware authorship priors: allocating fractional credit to first authors (\(w_1 \approx 0.50\)), corresponding authors (\(w_c \approx 0.35\)), and distributed middle authors (\(w_m \approx 0.15 / [N-2]\)). This reflects true laboratory contribution norms in biomedical, engineering, and collaborative computer science literature.

04

Distribution-Free Split-Conformal Prediction Sets

Upgrade heuristic confidence score cutoffs to finite-sample split-conformal calibration. Given a user-specified significance level \(\alpha \in (0, 1)\), the asset outputs a dynamic prediction set \(\mathcal{C}_{1-\alpha}(q)\) with mathematically guaranteed coverage \(P(\text{Relevant Candidate} \in \mathcal{C}_{1-\alpha}(q)) \ge 1 - \alpha\).

05

Automated Cross-Encoder NLI Faithfulness Verification

Introduce a lightweight Natural Language Inference (NLI) model (e.g., deberta-v3-small-nli) as an automated guardrail over System-2 RAG answers. Each synthesized claim is treated as a hypothesis verified against the cited abstract premise; assertions lacking strict entailment (\(P(\text{entailment}) < 0.85\)) are excised prior to advisor presentation to mathematically preclude hallucination.
